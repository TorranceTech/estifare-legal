Features

Everything a professional driver needs to close the ride

From the first quote to the receipt that brings the passenger back — EstiFare handles the business side of every trip.

Instant Estimates

Type a destination and get distance, drive time, and a fair price in seconds — powered by Apple Maps routing and your own rates.

Send Quotes

Share a professional ride quote by message, WhatsApp, or email — with an optional scheduled pickup date and time. Close the ride before the trip.

Branded PDF Receipts

Invoice-style receipts with your logo, company info, and payment options — professionally aligned and named by trip date for easy filing.

Earnings Dashboard

Today, this week, and this month at a glance — plus monthly miles, ready for tax season. Your numbers, always with you.

Driver Profiles

Save separate pricing profiles for different vehicles, companies, or markets — and switch between them in one tap.

Get Paid Your Way

Show your PayPal, Venmo, Cash App, or Zelle right on the receipt. Person-to-person payment — EstiFare takes zero commission.
